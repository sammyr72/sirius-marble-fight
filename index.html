How a fight goes

The short version, in the order it happens.

  1. 1

    Set the table

    The arena sits on your real table in passthrough, about forty centimetres across, with robots at 1:18 scale. A roulette wheel picks the place. Online, both headsets spin the same wheel and land on the same arena.

  2. 2

    Draw two marbles

    Each fighter gets one, drawn from the collection you own. You don’t choose; you fight with what you drew. No two marbles look alike.

  3. 3

    Fight

    The left stick walks your robot. Hold the grip and your hand flies a fist. A faster swing hits harder. Knock the other robot off the table, out of the ring, or out cold.

  4. 4

    First to three

    A marble that loses a round turns to steel. Win the match and you keep a marble, no purchase needed.

Small things we fussed over

Things nobody asked for. They’re all in.

Dents
The fist pushes the robot’s plastic in along its path, so the dent matches the punch.
Weight
The fists are steel on a plastic body. A swing carries its weight, and a miss drags you round with it.
Wobbly heads
Take enough hits to the head and the robot gets woozy. It staggers more and goes down easier.
Slow-mo
When a round ends, time crawls, the room dims and the sound sinks. The camera never moves. It’s your head.
Barks
The robots yell when they’re hit. The voices are made up on the spot: shrill, flat or oddly cheerful.
Footprints
Sand, dust, gravel and clay take a print, sized to the robot. Big Bots crack the concrete.

One robot, many paint jobs

The robot is a faceless, white-panelled toy android, and you can repaint it in any colour.

The bots have temperaments. Some box, some shove, and some just lean on you until you run out of table. Every one has a name, and a few are huge.

There’s a console on the table, too

An old CRT with a handle on the side. It’s where you pick fights, open marble packs and change the lights. It also hides a few games of its own, played with the marbles you’ve won.

> THE CIRCUIT

A ladder of named bots, from a cautious defender up to a Big Boss. Beat it and the Gauntlet opens: three at once, swarms of tiny bots, a pair of giants.

> RINGER

Proper old-fashioned marbles. A ten-foot ring, thirteen marbles in a cross, and surfaces you have to read: carpet, asphalt, blacktop. Knock them out and keep what you win.

> LABYRINTH

A wooden tilt maze folded into one long corridor, with forty-two numbered gates to pass in order, and holes in between.

> ASMR TABLE

A tray of steel, ivory, stone or hardwood marbles, and the sound of them rolling. The quietest thing in the game.

> THE GYM

A heavy bag, a double-end bag, a sparring dummy and an 80s cabinet with a neon sign. Warm up before you fight.

> COZY ROOM

Not into passthrough? Swap your room for a softly lit one with a couch, a rug and a window at dusk. The game stays on the table.
